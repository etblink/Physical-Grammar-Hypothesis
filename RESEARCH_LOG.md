# RESEARCH LOG

This file preserves the chronological conceptual history of PGH, including discarded ideas and changes in interpretation.

The log is intentionally descriptive. Entries record what was considered, not what has been established as true.

## 2026-08-28 — Origin of the Physical Grammar Hypothesis

### Context

A discussion about the use of different mathematical “languages” in foundational physics raised a speculative question:

> What if the rules of linguistics, understood at a sufficiently deep generative level, are identical with the rules of the physical universe?

The initial formulation focused on a tension between mathematical structure as representation and mathematical structure as potentially constitutive of physical law.

### Important correction

The discussion distinguished two claims that must not be conflated:

1. a formal structure may be too generic to count as evidence of independent convergence between theories;
2. that same genericity does **not** logically imply that the structure is physically unreal.

This distinction motivated a new question:

> Could some representation-generic structure be physically constitutive even though its recurrence cannot establish independent theoretical convergence?

No FCP result was changed by this question.

### First strong formulation

The provisional strong hypothesis became:

\[
P \cong W(G)/{\sim},
\]

with the additional requirement that `G` be:

- generative;
- nontrivially exclusive;
- representation-independent;
- free of hidden physical laws;
- comparatively compact;
- capable of yielding an independently derived restriction.

### Null hypothesis retained

The live null hypothesis remains that grammatical structure belongs to representation and that independent physical laws perform the substantive selection of physical possibility.

### Initial five-part conceptual scaffold

The first candidate notions were:

```text
DISTINCTION
BOUNDARY
COMPOSITION
TRANSFORMATION
EQUIVALENCE
```

These were chosen only as a conceptual scaffold.

### Reduction attempt

A possible reduction was identified:

```text
DISTINGUISH
COMPOSE
IDENTIFY
```

with the conjecture that:

- boundary may arise from partial composability;
- transformation may be representable through composition;
- context may arise through compositional embedding;
- equivalence may be represented or perhaps derived through contextual substitutability.

No reduction has yet been proven.

### Analogies deliberately quarantined

Several tempting parallels were identified but explicitly **not** promoted to derivations:

1. matched interfaces may resemble conservation-like bookkeeping;
2. derivational order may resemble temporal order;
3. contextual equivalence may resemble gauge or representation equivalence;
4. invariance under arbitrary renaming may resemble symmetry.

Current status of all four:

```text
ANALOGY_ONLY = YES
PHYSICAL_DERIVATION = NO
```

### Anti-triviality requirement

The project recognized an immediate danger: any physical theory can in principle be encoded into a formal grammar if the production rules are allowed to contain the entire theory.

Therefore a useful PGH must pass a no-smuggling test and produce exclusions or consequences not independently inserted into its rules or semantics.

### Translation-invariance idea

A possible deep criterion was identified:

> Fundamental grammatical structure should survive faithful translation between genuinely different mathematical surface languages.

This was recorded as a methodological conjecture rather than evidence for PGH.

### Repository decision

The hypothesis was deliberately **not** opened as an FCP branch.

Reason:

- PGH is not yet source-bound;
- it has no established physical consequence;
- it may eventually challenge or reinterpret distinctions that FCP uses methodologically;
- prematurely placing it inside FCP could bias its formulation or give it scientific status it has not earned.

A separate repository was therefore created:

```text
etblink/Physical-Grammar-Hypothesis
```

with status:

```text
PROJECT_CLASS = SPECULATIVE_FOUNDATIONAL_RESEARCH
FCP_RELATIONSHIP = INDEPENDENT_INCUBATION
FCP_FRAMEWORK_STATUS = NONE
CANONICAL_EFFECT_ON_FCP = NONE
```

### Repository-opening decision

The project adopted lightweight provenance rather than the full FCP governance architecture.

Core records:

- repository opening charter;
- current state;
- hypothesis definition;
- primitive ledger;
- nontriviality tests;
- research log;
- handoffs.

### Failed-derivation policy

The project adopted:

```text
FAILED_DERIVATIONS_ARE_FIRST_CLASS_RESULTS = YES
```

The goal is to prevent attractive but circular arguments from disappearing as the hypothesis evolves.

### Current stopping point

No formal PGH derivation has yet been attempted or accepted.

The next recommended research task is:

```text
PGH0_MINIMAL_GRAMMAR_CHALLENGE
```

Question:

> Can DISTINGUISH, COMPOSE, and IDENTIFY be defined without hidden physical content, can any one of them be eliminated, and does the surviving structure imply any nontrivial exclusion before a physical interpretation is imposed?

## 2026-08-28 — Repository scalability and navigation foundation

### Reason

Before beginning the first formal PGH derivation, the project established a lightweight scalable repository spine so future growth would not depend on chat context or later metadata reconstruction.

Operation:

```text
PGH_REPOSITORY_SCALABILITY_AND_NAVIGATION_FOUNDATION
```

### Scientific boundary

The operation was infrastructure-only:

```text
SCIENTIFIC_CHANGE = NONE
HYPOTHESIS_CHANGE = NONE
PRIMITIVE_ADJUDICATION = NONE
NEW_DERIVATION = NONE
NEW_SOURCE_SEARCH = NONE
```

The recommended `PGH0_MINIMAL_GRAMMAR_CHALLENGE` remained unstarted.

### Authority hierarchy

The project adopted:

```text
GIT = PROVENANCE_AUTHORITY
CANONICAL_MARKDOWN_ARTIFACTS = RESEARCH_AND_GOVERNANCE_AUTHORITY
STRUCTURED_NAVIGATION_LAYER = DERIVED_NAVIGATION_ONLY
```

### Stable IDs and registries

The project established durable IDs for operations, questions, research objects, derivations, failures, and candidate grammars, together with:

- an operation registry;
- a research-object registry;
- an open-question registry;
- a derived canonical index;
- a navigation schema and checker.

The opening ten questions were assigned stable IDs `PGH-Q-0001` through `PGH-Q-0010`.

The current D/C/I candidate shell was assigned:

```text
PGH-GRAM-0001
```

This identity does not establish minimality, coherence, or physical significance.

### Assumption ancestry

Future substantive derivations are required to expose primitive, rule, lemma, semantic, physical, and source dependencies.

The purpose is to make hidden physical imports mechanically traceable.

### Failure preservation

Failure classes were standardized without scoring them.

Historical failures must remain preserved rather than being silently repaired out of the record.

### Handoff discipline

Future major handoffs now require a machine-readable capsule containing a `do_not_assume` list.

This is intended to protect successor instances from promoting analogies into established results.

### Source boundary

A reserved `sources/` area was created while preserving:

```text
SOURCE_BOUND_STATUS = NOT_YET_SOURCE_BOUND
```

No source search or literature admission occurred.

### Result

The repository is now prepared for larger-scale research without changing the PGH scientific state.

The next recommended operation remains:

```text
PGH0_MINIMAL_GRAMMAR_CHALLENGE
```
