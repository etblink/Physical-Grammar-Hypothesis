## Purpose

Classify ways a future grammar might generate proper local admissibility supports without treating the support table itself as primitive.

The map is a discovery artifact, not a ranking of physical theories.

## Family map

| Family | Proper-support power | Non-table-driven in fixed formalism | Current unearned input | R2 priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arbitrary support table | Yes | No | Entire support | Reject |
| Unrestricted local formula/predicate | Yes | No in explanatory sense | Rule language / predicate interpretation | Reject as endpoint |
| Equational support | Yes | Yes | Signature and equations | Promising conditional |
| Typing/interface matching | Yes | Yes | Type/interface system | Promising conditional |
| Rewrite/coherence consistency | Yes | Yes | Rewrite/equivalence/coherence premises | Promising conditional |
| Symmetry/invariance support | Conditional | Yes | Symmetry action and invariant selector | Secondary/control |
| Empirical/operational support | Yes | Often | Physical possibility/response structure | Reject as PGH origin |

## Central distinction

The relevant hierarchy is:

\[
\text{table}
\;<\;
\text{formula in unrestricted language}
\;<\;
\text{rule in independently restricted language}
\;<\;
\text{rule language generated by deeper structure}.
\]

Only the latter two levels have a chance to improve explanatory status.

## Equational family

Given a fixed signature `Sigma` and equations `E`, a local support may be defined as tuples satisfying selected term equalities.

Advantages:

- compact rule reuse;
- structural rather than tuple-by-tuple definition;
- mature mathematical semantics;
- compatibility with compositional grammar.

Burden:

```text
WHY_SIGMA?
WHY_E?
ARE_SIGMA_AND_E_FIXED_BEFORE_TARGET_PHYSICS?
```

## Typing/interface family

A type system may reject tuples whose interfaces do not match admissible composition.

Advantages:

- local well-formedness naturally tied to composition;
- may make boundary/type structure generative rather than enumerative.

Burden:

```text
TYPE_SYSTEM_DERIVED = NO_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE
TYPE_DECLARATIONS_CAN_ENCODE_ADMISSIBILITY = YES
```

## Coherence/rewrite family

A local configuration may be admissible only if alternative derivations agree or normalize consistently.

Advantages:

- compact non-table-driven exclusion;
- strong frozen-corpus prior art;
- directly related to the project's representation-coherence work.

Burden:

```text
REWRITE_RULES_FIXED_INDEPENDENTLY = UNESTABLISHED
PHYSICAL_SIGNIFICANCE_OF_COHERENCE = UNESTABLISHED
```

## Symmetry family

A fixed grammar automorphism action may require support invariance.

Advantages:

- representation discipline;
- can exclude noninvariant candidate supports.

Burden:

```text
SYMMETRY_ACTION_ORIGIN = UNESTABLISHED
INVARIANCE_ALONE_UNDERDETERMINES_SUPPORT = YES
```

## Empirical/operational family

Operational principles and constructor-style possibility statements can define nontrivial local structure and are important comparison programs.

For anchored PGH they are not an acceptable explanatory endpoint if the same physical possibilities are what grammar is supposed to derive.
