## Why SG4 is distinct

The active failure is not merely lack of a compact syntax. It is universal realizability of arbitrary response maps/supports/distributions by lawful models of the same bridge-enriched theory.

SG4 is relevant only insofar as it studies restrictions that arise **uniformly from process composition/probabilistic structure across models**.

It is not privileged because it is modern, categorical, or physically successful.
