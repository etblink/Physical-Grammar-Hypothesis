## Purpose

Provide the weakest response-relevant semantic realization of `PGH-GRAM-0010` that preserves its entire observable model class while assigning **no physical ontology** to its internal latent sources.

## Formal source class

Let

\[
\mathcal T_{\mathrm{ind}}
\]

be the class of binary observed joint distributions generated by the fixed independent-source triangle grammar:

\[
p(a,b,c)=\sum_{u,v,w}p(u)p(v)p(w)
 p_A(a\mid u,w)p_B(b\mid u,v)p_C(c\mid v,w).
\]

The latent variables `U,V,W` and all local kernels are internal formal witnesses/model data.

## Prospective empirical roles

Introduce three binary record-valued empirical variable roles

\[
\mathcal A,\mathcal B,\mathcal C.
\]

No real physical variables are selected by this schema.

The roles are symmetric at formal scope under the same observed-node permutations that are automorphisms/presentations of `PGH-GRAM-0010`.

## Latent-ontology neutrality

The bridge makes no map

```text
U -> physical source 1
V -> physical source 2
W -> physical source 3
```

and asserts no physical causal graph.

Instead, `U,V,W` remain internal variables appearing in an existential representation condition that defines the observable model class.

Thus the physical candidate content transferred by the bridge is simply:

\[
\boxed{
p_{\mathrm{emp}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B,\mathcal C)
\in\mathcal T_{\mathrm{ind}}
}
\]

conditional on a later valid empirical instantiation of the three roles.

A future empirical distribution may be tested for compatibility with the model class without first asserting that any reconstructed latent source is a real physical cause.

## Source-independence accounting

The statement

```text
p(U,V,W)=p(U)p(V)p(W)
```

is part of formal grammar identity `PGH-GRAM-0010`.

The bridge does **not** separately assert:

```text

```
