## Purpose

`PGH-SR` is the narrow research target that remains after subtracting the extensive formal and foundational prior-art neighborhoods identified in the frozen 37-source landscape.

It does not replace historical `PGH-S` as the umbrella hypothesis family. It sharpens the part of `PGH-S` that remains scientifically unresolved for this project.

## Residual statement

There exists a compact generative structure `G` together with a physically justified equivalence relation `~phys` on its adequate presentations such that:

1. `~phys` is selected by a non-result-directed criterion of physical irrelevance rather than by choosing the equivalence that produces a desired law;
2. physically possible structures correspond, up to that equivalence, to the well-formed structures generated by `G`;
3. the substantive exclusion of physically impossible structures is carried by grammatical well-formedness/coherence after the quotient;
4. no independent physical law set remains outside `G` and `~phys` doing the substantive selection work.

Schematic target:

\[
P \cong W(G)/{\sim_{phys}},
\]

with the additional negative requirement:

\[
L_{independent}=\varnothing
\]

in the sense that no separate law-like selector `L` is required to recover the substantive physical possibility set.

The second expression is schematic bookkeeping, not a claim that physical laws literally form a set that must be empty.

## Two necessary residual burdens

### R1 — Physical Irrelevance Selector

There must exist a criterion `C_phys` capable of deciding which differences between formal presentations are physically representational rather than physically real.

It must satisfy:

```text
R1_NON_RESULT_DIRECTED = REQUIRED
R1_REPRESENTATION_ROBUST = REQUIRED
R1_DISCRIMINATES_COMPETING_EQUIVALENCES = REQUIRED
R1_DOES_NOT_DEFINE_PHYSICAL_AS_WHATEVER_PRESERVES_TARGET_LAW = REQUIRED
R1_HIDDEN_PHYSICAL_LAW = FORBIDDEN
```

R1 is immediate and unresolved.

### R2 — Law Exhaustion

After the physical equivalence quotient is fixed, the grammar itself must account for substantive physical exclusion.

It must satisfy:

```text
R2_INDEPENDENT_LAW_SET_DOES_SELECTION = FORBIDDEN
R2_SUBSTANTIVE_EXCLUSION_FROM_GRAMMAR = REQUIRED
R2_NO_SEMANTIC_SMUGGLING = REQUIRED
R2_AT_LEAST_ONE_INDEPENDENT_PHYSICAL_RESTRICTION = REQUIRED
```

R2 is live but deferred until R1 or another independently justified route supplies a meaningful physical-content quotient.

## Distinction from the live null

The live null remains:

\[
P=\operatorname{Select}_L(W(G)),
\]

where an independent physical law set or equivalent selection principle `L` performs the substantive selection.

`PGH-SR` differs only if:

```text
R1 = SATISFIED
AND
R2 = SATISFIED
```

If R1 fails, representation-independent grammatical necessity has not been physically identified.

If R2 fails, grammar is only a representational wrapper around independent physical law.

Either failure is sufficient to block strong PGH at the corresponding candidate scope.

## Relationship to existing programs

### Formal grammar

Supplies generation and well-formedness concepts; does not establish physical identity.

### Universal algebra / algebraic theories / rewriting / coherence

Supply mature formal machinery for quotienting, interpretation, and coherent presentation; do not select the physically privileged quotient.

### Structural realism

Gives structure or relations ontological/epistemic priority; does not by itself establish generative grammatical exhaustion of law.

### Theoretical equivalence and duality

Supply multiple candidate equivalence notions; their plurality is part of R1 rather than a solution to it.

### Categorical/process physics and reconstructions

Demonstrate that compositional or compact structural principles can have physical content; do not establish the PGH-SR no-independent-law condition.

### Constructor theory

Centers possible/impossible transformations; does not by itself identify possibility with well-formedness under a physically selected representation quotient.

### Laws as constraints

Supports non-dynamical/modal conceptions of law; does not show that law is exhausted by grammar.

### Mathematical Universe Hypothesis

Places mathematics at the ontological level; does not supply R1 or R2 for PGH.

## Failure conditions

`PGH-SR` should be downgraded if any of the following becomes unavoidable:

1. every proposed `C_phys` is circular, result-directed, or theory-dependent in exactly the substantive way PGH was meant to explain;
2. no robust notion of physically irrelevant presentation can be selected among competing mathematically adequate equivalences;
3. an independent law set remains necessary after grammar and physical equivalence are fixed;
4. the semantic/realization map carries the actual physical selection;
5. all candidate physical exclusions are restatements of imported laws;
6. the residual formulation cannot be sharpened into a candidate with concrete failure exposure.
