## Purpose

Restate the anchored PGH R2 burden after adjudicating that mandatory meta-derivation is not a canonical truth-condition.

This object does not weaken the no-independent-law requirement. It changes only the admissibility boundary for the candidate grammar itself.

## Architecture

Let

```text
A_ref = law-free empirical reference/contact anchor
G = preregistered candidate primitive grammar satisfying PGH-OBJ-0020
W(G) = grammar-generated well-formed structures
P = physical possibility/content if a physical bridge is later established
```

The live architecture is

\[
A_{ref}+G\longrightarrow W(G)\xrightarrow{\text{candidate consequences}}\cdots
\]

with no independent substantive selector permitted to perform the work claimed for `G`.

## Candidate-grammar burden

`G` need not be derived from a meta-grammar merely to enter candidacy.

It must satisfy `PGH-OBJ-0020`, including:

```text
PRE_TARGET_FIXATION
RESTRICTED_HYPOTHESIS_CLASS

DECLARED_PRIMITIVES

```

## Status of former R2A

The research question

> can selective structural structure be generated from something smaller?

remains scientifically interesting and may strengthen a candidate.

But:

```text
R2A_META_DERIVATION = OPTIONAL_STRENGTHENING_NOT_MANDATORY_TRUTH_CONDITION
```

A failure to derive `G` from a deeper principle is not by itself a failure of PGH.

## Mandatory R2 burden

The mandatory burden is now cleanly separated:

### R2C — candidate grammar admissibility

```text
QUESTION = Does a preregistered G pass PGH-OBJ-0020?
STATUS = NO_SPECIFIC_G_TESTED_YET
```

### R2B — physical law exhaustion

```text
QUESTION = After A_ref is fixed, does G generate substantive physical selection without an independent law set?
STATUS = UNSATISFIED
```

R2B cannot begin until at least one concrete `G` passes formal candidate screening.

## Null

The anchored null remains

\[
P=\operatorname{Select}_L(W(G),A_{ref}),
\]

where independent selector `L` performs the substantive work.

If `L` is required, strong anchored PGH fails for that candidate.
