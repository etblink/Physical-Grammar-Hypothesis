## Purpose

This object records the source-bound accounting needed to test compositional stochastic/causal mechanisms against the current PGH bridge-response universal-realizability failure.

It does not select a physical grammar or causal model.

## Parametric-selection principle

The schema distinguishes a mechanism family from a fixed structural member of that family.

A statement such as

```text
CAUSAL_STRUCTURE_RESTRICTS_MODELS
```

is incomplete for PGH explanatory accounting unless the restrictive structural input is identified.

A family containing both sparse and complete/highly connected graphs may contain members with very different model-class exclusion power.

Thus any later causal grammar candidate must bind its graph/wiring structure to provenance rather than treating it as invisible semantic background.
