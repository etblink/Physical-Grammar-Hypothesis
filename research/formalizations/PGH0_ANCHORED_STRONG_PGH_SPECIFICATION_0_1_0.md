## Why this is a new object

`PGH-OBJ-0012` is not a rewrite of `PGH-OBJ-0008`.

The original residual strong target required R1 to supply a physically justified irrelevance selector. The completed R1 sequence did not satisfy that burden.

The anchored successor changes the explanatory boundary explicitly:

> minimal physical reference/contact may be irreducible semantic input, provided it remains law-free; substantive physical law and modal selection must still be generated by grammar rather than by an independent law set or by the semantic anchor.

This change is scientifically material and therefore receives a new research-object identity.

## Core structure

Let:

```text
G = candidate generative grammar
A_ref = law-free physical reference/contact anchor
W(G) = well-formed structures generated by G
```

The anchor may include a context/record interface of the form

\[
A_{ref}=(C,R,\iota_C,\rho),
\]

subject to the law-free firewall below.

The successor hypothesis is schematically:

\[
A_{ref}+G\longrightarrow P,
\]

where `P` denotes physical possibility/content only if the substantive exclusions and response regularities are generated by `G` after the anchor is held fixed.

## Law-free anchor firewall

The primitive anchor may supply:

- empirical reference/ostension for selected probe or interface tokens;
- record-label reference;
- semantic transport data needed to compare adequate formal presentations.

It may not primitively supply:

```text
ALLOWED_RESPONSE_RELATION
RESPONSE_FUNCTION
PROBABILITY_OR_AMPLITUDE_RULE
COMPLETE_POSSIBLE_IMPOSSIBLE_SET
TARGET_EMPIRICAL_PARTITION
INDEPENDENT_MODAL_SELECTION_RULE
EMPIRICAL_SUCCESS_AS_AN_INTRINSIC_SELECTOR
```

Operational criterion:

```text
A_REF_MUST_BE_COMPATIBLE_WITH_MULTIPLE_EMPIRICALLY_INCOMPATIBLE_RESPONSE_LAWS_WHILE_HELD_FIXED
```

Failure of that criterion means substantive law has entered through semantics.

## Explicit explanatory cost

The successor does not claim:

```text
GRAMMAR_DERIVES_PHYSICAL_REFERENCE = NO

```

Physical reference/contact is treated as primitive or externally anchored at current scope.

This is an admitted explanatory boundary, not a derived result.

## Strong residual burden

Despite that concession, the successor preserves the central no-independent-law demand.

After `A_ref` is fixed:

```text
INDEPENDENT_SUBSTANTIVE_LAW_SET = FORBIDDEN
SUBSTANTIVE_PHYSICAL_EXCLUSION_FROM_G = REQUIRED
NO_SEMANTIC_SMUGGLING = REQUIRED
AT_LEAST_ONE_INDEPENDENT_PHYSICAL_RESTRICTION_FROM_G = REQUIRED
```

Thus the live null remains:

\[
P=\operatorname{Select}_L(W(G),A_{ref}),
\]

where `L` is an independent law-like selector doing the substantive work.

`PGH-OBJ-0012` differs only if the grammar itself eliminates the need for such an `L`.

## Representation requirement

The anchor and grammar must admit adequate translation between mathematical presentations such that declared empirical record profiles commute when the translation conditions are satisfied.

Representation robustness is required for a candidate physical realization, but robustness alone does not establish physical privilege.

## Empirical-interface closure

A declared law-free empirical seed may be expanded by response-independent constructors mechanically inherited from the grammar formation structure:

\[
C_G^*(C_0)=Cl_{E(G)}(C_0).
\]

Equal-closure seeds may be treated as generator-equivalent for all downstream structure that factors through the closure.

Neither seed minimality nor generic closed-substructure canonicality is a physical privilege principle.

## Failure conditions

`PGH-OBJ-0012` fails at candidate scope if any of the following becomes unavoidable:

1. the supposedly law-free anchor must be enriched with substantive response/possibility rules to connect the grammar to physics;
2. the grammar generates no independent substantive physical restriction once the anchor is held fixed;
3. an external law-like selector remains necessary to recover the physical possibility or response space;
4. all apparent grammar-derived exclusions are already encoded in the anchor, constructor extraction, or realization map;
5. representation transport requires importing the target physics;
6. empirical success can be obtained only by choosing the anchor or grammar after seeing the desired law;
7. no concrete failure-exposing physical candidate can ever be instantiated.
