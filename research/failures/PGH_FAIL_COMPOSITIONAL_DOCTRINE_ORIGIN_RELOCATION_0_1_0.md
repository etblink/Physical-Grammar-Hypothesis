## Failed move

> Structural permission `P` follows canonically from compositional doctrine `D`; therefore `P` has been explained by PGH.

This inference fails unless `D` is itself independently fixed by accepted PGH structure.

## Reason

Different disciplined doctrines generate different structural packages.

Examples at purely formal scope:

```text
BARE_MONOIDAL -> no exchange/copy/discard
SYMMETRIC_MONOIDAL -> exchange but no copy/discard
CARTESIAN -> diagonal + terminal deletion + exchange
COCARTESIAN -> codiagonal + initial creation + exchange
BICARTESIAN -> both product and coproduct packages
```

Each stronger doctrine can be mathematically canonical relative to its own universal properties while remaining one member of a plurality of possible doctrines.

## Universal-encoding control

A desired structural permission can also be made primitive by adjoining an appropriately typed generating morphism or structural axiom to a presentation.

Therefore existence of a doctrine realizing a target package is not evidence that the package was generated non-result-directly.
