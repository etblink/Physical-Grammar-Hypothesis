# PGH-FAIL-0022 — Doctrine-Only Grammar Shell Incompleteness as a Candidacy Blocker

## Status

```text
FAILURE_ID = PGH-FAIL-0022
STATUS = FAILED_PRESERVED
FAILURE_CLASS = METHODOLOGICAL_OVERCONSTRAINT
```

## Failed claim

> A compositional doctrine cannot be a complete formal candidate grammar unless a finite non-target-directed generator seed is also fixed.

## Why the claim fails

A grammar can be specified axiomatically by the class of structures satisfying it:

\[
W(G)=\{X:X\models G\}.
\]

No finite generator presentation is required for that model class to be formally defined, exclusionary, compressive, and counterexample-exposing.

The primitive grammar admissibility standard does not require a generator seed.

## What remains valid from the rejected argument

Two safeguards survive:

1. **Target-specific extensions are new content.** Adding arbitrary generators, relations, equations, or response rules after seeing a target cannot be credited to the unchanged doctrine.
2. **Semantic/model selection can smuggle physics.** If a later physical bridge picks a model or interpretation solely because it reproduces the desired law, the doctrine has not generated that law.

Thus:

```text
SEED_NOT_REQUIRED_FOR_FORMAL_CANDIDACY = YES
TARGET_SPECIFIC_PRESENTATION_DATA_REQUIRES_NEW_ADJUDICATION = YES
SEMANTIC_MODEL_SELECTION_REMAINS_A_NO_SMUGGLING_RISK = YES
```

## Consequence

Doctrine-level candidates may proceed to physical-bridge feasibility testing without pretending that a finite free presentation is the grammar itself.
