## Next boundary

The project must specify a scientifically defensible stopping rule distinguishing:

1. a compact primitive grammar postulate;
2. a disguised list of target laws;
3. an arbitrary formal choice with no predictive burden.

Until that boundary is adjudicated, adding another meta-doctrine would risk manufacturing regress rather than testing PGH.
