## Landscape result

The frozen landscape shows substantial prior-art overlap with the formal machinery PGH has independently developed.

Most importantly:

- contextual quotienting and congruence/factorization live in mature universal-algebra and algebraic-theory traditions;
- rewrite/confluence theory already studies equivalence through alternative derivations and normal forms;
- Mac Lane coherence provides direct theorem-family prior art for the project's parse/coherence–associativity result;
- operads and multicategories provide mature variable-arity/tree-composition machinery;
- Lawvere theories, monads, and related algebraic semantics provide established syntax/interpretation/factorization frameworks;
- graphical calculi provide rigorous presentation-invariant compositional reasoning;
- philosophy of physics already contains multiple non-equivalent notions of theoretical equivalence and duality;
- structural realism, categorical/process physics, operational reconstructions, constructor theory, laws-as-constraints, and mathematical-universe theses occupy nearby foundational territory.

Therefore current PGH formal results must not be represented as novel mathematics merely because they were obtained independently inside PGH.

## Open-question consequences

### PGH-Q-0008

The question of faithful translation between mathematical surface languages has a substantial existing theoretical-equivalence/duality literature. It remains open for PGH because no one universal equivalence criterion is supplied by that literature.

### PGH-Q-0014

Variable-arity and relational compositional formalisms are already well developed through operads, multicategories, higher categories, and related structures. The live burden is physical privilege/interpretation, not mathematical availability.

### PGH-Q-0015

Known notions of equivalence, functorial semantics, quotients, and duality provide candidate translation structures. They do not automatically determine which translation is appropriate for a physical grammar.
