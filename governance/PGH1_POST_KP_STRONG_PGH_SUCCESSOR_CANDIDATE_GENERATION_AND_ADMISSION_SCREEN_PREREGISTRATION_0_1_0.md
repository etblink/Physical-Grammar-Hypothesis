## Purpose

Generate a finite, explicitly target-free family of post-Kp successor **candidate packages** and apply the A0-A9 strong-PGH admission standard to each package.

The operation may return zero, one, or multiple admitted successors.

It must not inspect empirical target metadata to choose among candidates and must stop before target discovery even if a candidate passes.

## Generation principle

Candidate packages are generated from **pre-existing target-free structural motifs already present in the repository**, together with the newly qualified candidate-identity and scope standards.

No lane is privileged because it resembles successful physics or because it might avoid the old Kp failure.

The lanes are chosen to span the project’s surviving structural possibilities and its strongest controls.
