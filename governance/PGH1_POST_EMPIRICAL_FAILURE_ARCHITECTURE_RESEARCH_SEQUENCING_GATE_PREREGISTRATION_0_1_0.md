## Purpose

Decide which **target-free research direction, if any**, remains scientifically justified after OP-0096 retired the exhaustive minimal sparse-DAG path architecture.

The gate may select a research problem or a bounded source/formal gap. It may also conclude that no current strong-PGH successor direction has enough independent motivation to justify continued active candidate generation.

The gate must not manufacture motivation from Kp or HURDAT2 behavior.

## Governing distinction

The canonical stopping-rule adjudication remains binding:

```text
MANDATORY_META_DERIVATION_OF_EVERY_PRIMITIVE = NOT_A_PGH_REQUIREMENT
A_DECLARED_PRIMITIVE_G = PERMITTED_AS_CANDIDATE_CONTENT_IF_IT_PASSES_STRICT_ADMISSION
```

Therefore this sequencing gate may **not** restore the superseded requirement that every primitive architecture be derived from a deeper one.

But permissibility is not a reason to spend empirical effort.

The gate must distinguish:

```text
CANDIDATE_ADMISSIBILITY
from
INDEPENDENT_THEORETICAL_MOTIVATION_FOR_A_RESEARCH_DIRECTION
```
