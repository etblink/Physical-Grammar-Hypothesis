## Families

### F0 — universal physical scope

```text
S(e) = TRUE
```

Question: does explicit universal scope remain the cleanest currently qualified strong-PGH-compatible scope architecture because it introduces no separate selector?

The gate may not infer that a future grammar is true or viable merely because universal scope is methodologically clean.

### F1 — grammar/bridge-internal structural scope

Applicability is claimed to follow from structure already internal to `G` and/or `J`.

Questions:

1. does the frozen literature show that formal/model structure by itself normally earns a physical target relation?
2. can F1 remain formally possible under PGH even if no source-qualified concrete mechanism currently exists?
3. what evidence would be required before F1 could receive physical scope credit?

### F2 — primitive law-free observational / semantic scope

Applicability is fixed by primitive reference, measurement, observational, or semantic conditions that do not themselves state substantive response laws.

Questions:

1. can such conditions qualify as interface facts without becoming physical law selectors?
2. does the source corpus support automatic domain selection from semantic relation alone?
3. where is the boundary at which F2 becomes `PGH-FAIL-0037`?

### F3 — independently established physical-sector / effective-regime scope

Applicability is determined by substantive physical conditions such as scale separation, heavy/light degrees of freedom, symmetry, equilibrium, coupling regime, causal/environmental assumptions, or other prior physical structure.

Questions:

1. is F3 scientifically legitimate in ordinary/effective physics?
2. does F3 receive strong-PGH law-exhaustion credit if those conditions are independent of the grammar?
3. could F3 become compatible only if the scope-generating physical structure were itself generated/accounted for within the PGH candidate?

### F4 — higher-level grammar / meta-selector

A higher-level grammar assigns lower-level grammars or applicability domains.

Questions:

1. does F4 resolve scope origin or merely move it upward?
2. what conditions would make the recursion terminate non-arbitrarily?
3. does unrestricted/meta-enumerative selection fail by the same universal-encoding logic?
