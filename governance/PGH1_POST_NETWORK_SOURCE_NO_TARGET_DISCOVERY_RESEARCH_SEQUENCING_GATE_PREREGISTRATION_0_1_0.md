## Independent-motivation criteria

A route that authorizes active new work must pass all applicable criteria:

### M1 — pre-result ancestry

The specific methodological or scientific reason must be traceable to a canonical artifact whose scientific content predates the OP-0110 no-target result.

A generic statement such as “more search might find something” does not pass.

### M2 — counterfactual choice

Ask:

> Would this exact next research route have been scientifically defensible if OP-0110 had found one eligible target?

If no, the route is outcome-dependent and fails.

### M3 — candidate-identity firewall

No route may alter `G`, `J`, `S`, or `I` of `PGH-OBJ-0052` under the same identity.

A change to native binary requirements, field selection, role assignment, scope, or bridge creates a different future candidate and is outside this gate.

### M4 — no target-family privileging

A search expansion cannot be justified by a known portal, domain, dataset family, institution, or record type appearing especially promising among TGT-001..TGT-039.

### M5 — finite prospective closure

Any discovery-method expansion must admit a finite, deterministic stopping rule before renewed target search begins. “Search until something qualifies” fails.

### M6 — no dependence information

No result, hint, terminology, or prior knowledge concerning candidate dependence, independence, source structure, correlation, or `T_ind` compatibility may motivate a route.

### M7 — explanatory-credit neutrality

Finding or failing to find a target does not count as evidence for or against the physical truth of `PGH-OBJ-0052` unless an actual admissible empirical test occurs.
