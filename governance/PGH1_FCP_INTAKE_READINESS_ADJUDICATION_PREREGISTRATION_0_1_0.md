## 5. Frozen readiness gates

The following gates are outcome-neutral and must be adjudicated independently.

### RG1 — stable candidate identity

There must be a frozen candidate object with explicit relation to historical failed identities and explicit revision boundaries.

### RG2 — non-generic primitive/object class

The candidate must contain more than generic mathematics, representation, graph notation, or statistical fitting machinery.

### RG3 — coherent model/configuration domain

The candidate must define a stable class of physical or prospectively physical possibilities rather than one fitted model or one target-specific equation.

### RG4 — FCP-0.2.1 criterion-C plausibility

There must be a serious source-bound case that the candidate's own architecture supplies a physically interpreted law or constraint on admissible physical possibilities, not merely a language for representing possibilities supplied by another theory.

This gate is about *intake plausibility*, not final `C=PASS`; FCP retains authority to reject the claim at taxonomy.

### RG5 — physical scope and interpretation burden

The candidate must expose a stable physical scope plus a nontrivial bridge/interpretation burden. Purely formal scope or representational relabeling without physical exposure is insufficient.

### RG6 — explicit empirical/observational burden

The candidate must state a prospective condition under which physical observations could count against it. Existing empirical success is not required.

### RG7 — source/provenance sufficiency for independent FCP intake

The PGH corpus must expose enough canonical primary artifacts and source-bound supporting provenance for FCP to reconstruct the candidate without silently importing PGH's own verdicts as independent validation.

PGH self-adjudication is primary proposal evidence, **not independent external confirmation**.

### RG8 — provisional K1–K10 mappability

The object must be sufficiently stable that FCP could prospectively map K1–K10 without inventing missing ontology, dynamics, realization, or empirical structure. `NONE`, `OPEN`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` remain valid eventual values.

### RG9 — principled distinctness question is adjudicable

There must be a concrete identity that FCP can compare against existing framework definitions. Distinctness is not presumed; the question must merely be well-posed enough for a taxonomy gate.

### RG10 — negative-history preservation

The failed `PGH-GRAM-0008` / Kp result must remain load-bearing provenance and must not be treated as superseded evidence in favor of the new candidate.

## 7. Required hostile checks

Before `R1` may be assigned, the adjudication must attempt to falsify readiness through at least these objections:

1. `PGH-GRAM-0010` is only a generic latent-variable/statistical model class with no PGH-specific physical-law content.
2. `PGH-OBJ-0051` merely relabels probability distributions and therefore fails physical interpretation.
3. universal `S=TRUE` is only a methodological exposure rule, not a stable physical scope.
4. `I` selects generic datasets rather than a physically meaningful interface.
5. the candidate's physical restriction actually comes from imported source-independence assumptions rather than from `G`.
6. the absence of a selected empirical target makes FCP intake premature.
7. the absence of empirical success makes FCP intake premature.
8. PGH's source corpus is too self-referential for FCP to independently reconstruct the proposal.
9. the post-Kp successor is an outcome-directed rescue of `PGH-GRAM-0008`.
10. the candidate cannot be distinguished from generic causal/Bayesian-network, latent-variable, GPTOPT, process-matrix, or method-tooling objects without importing target behavior.

Any surviving material objection must be recorded rather than averaged away.
