## Purpose

Test the first half of the post-meta-language residual burden:

```text
R2A = GENERATE_THE_SELECTIVE_STRUCTURAL_PACKAGE_NON_RESULT_DIRECTLY
```

The gate asks whether structural permissions that materially affect a rule language can arise from smaller compositional structure rather than being stipulated as object-logical rules.

The selective package is denoted

\[
\Theta.
\]

At this gate `Theta` may include permissions analogous to:

- exchange / ordering;
- contraction / copy;
- weakening / discard;
- typing/interface matching;
- coherence/equation structure.

No physical interpretation of these permissions is authorized.

## Central distinction

The gate must distinguish:

\[
\text{permission generated inside a fixed compositional doctrine}
\]

from

\[
\text{the compositional doctrine itself being generated or privileged}.
\]

A permission earns no R2A success merely because a stronger formalism makes it canonical.

## Locked test ladder

### T0 — bare strict monoidal composition

Use the free strict monoidal category on two object-generators `A,B` and no nonidentity morphism generators.

Test whether composition/tensor alone supplies:

```text
A -> A tensor A          [copy-like]
A -> I                   [discard-like]
A tensor B -> B tensor A [exchange-like]
```

No extra braiding, symmetry, diagonal, terminal map, or structural generator may be added.

### T1 — symmetric monoidal composition

Use the free symmetric monoidal category on `A,B` and no arity-changing morphism generators.

Test whether symmetry supplies exchange while still failing to supply copy/discard.

### T2 — cartesian composition

For a category with finite products, test the universal-property constructions

\[
\Delta_A = \langle 1_A,1_A\rangle:A\to A\times A
\]

and

\[
!_A:A\to 1.
\]

Test whether product symmetry supplies exchange and whether these maps are canonical relative to the finite-product doctrine.

### T3 — cocartesian plurality control

For a category with finite coproducts, compare the canonical package generated by coproduct/initial structure, including codiagonal

\[
\nabla_A:A+A\to A
\]

and the initial map

\[
0\to A.
\]

The purpose is to test whether universal-property generation uniquely selects the cartesian structural package or merely generates different packages relative to different doctrines.

### T4 — doctrine-level universal-encoding control

A desired permission may always be inserted by adjoining a generating morphism or structural axiom to a presentation.

Therefore:

```text
THERE_EXISTS_A_DOCTRINE_REALIZING_THETA
```

is not explanatory evidence unless the doctrine is fixed independently of the target `Theta`.

### T5 — physical firewall

No copy/discard/exchange map may be identified with a physical process, conservation law, quantum operation, causal primitive, or empirical law at this gate.
