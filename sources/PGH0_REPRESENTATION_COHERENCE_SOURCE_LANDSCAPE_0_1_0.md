## Executive result

The source landscape materially changes how PGH should describe its present state.

The following parts of the current program have clear, substantial prior art:

1. quotienting syntax/presentations by congruence or contextual equivalence;
2. factorization of interpretation/evaluation through quotients or algebraic theories;
3. confluence, normal forms, and coherence through rewrite systems;
4. associativity/coherence under changes of parenthesization;
5. variable-arity composition through operads/multicategories;
6. presentation-independent or diagrammatic reasoning in monoidal/category-theoretic settings;
7. multiple formal criteria for when two theories/presentations represent the same physical theory;
8. structuralist and invariance-based accounts of mathematical/physical identity;
9. process/compositional approaches to physical theories;
10. foundational programs deriving physical structure from compact operational/informational principles;
11. laws conceived as modal constraints rather than exclusively as time-evolution rules;
12. the thesis that physical reality may itself be mathematical structure.

Therefore the present PGH formal machinery must **not** be described as novel mathematics merely because it was independently rediscovered inside this project.

The source landscape does **not** establish that strong PGH already exists in the literature. It instead isolates a narrower unresolved residue: the conjunction of a generative-grammar interpretation of physical possibility with a non-result-directed rule for selecting which presentation distinctions are physically irrelevant, such that physical law emerges as well-formedness/coherence rather than as an independently imposed law set.

No novelty claim is authorized from this residual description.

## Lane-by-lane findings

### L1 — Universal algebra and quotient machinery

Birkhoff and the standard universal-algebra literature make clear that terms, equations, homomorphisms, congruences, free algebras, and quotients are mature machinery. Burris and Sankappanavar provide a direct modern route from term structure to congruence quotients and equational logic.

PGH overlap:

```text
PGH-DER-0001 = STRONG_FORMAL_PRIOR_ART_OVERLAP
PGH-DER-0004 = STRONG_FORMAL_PRIOR_ART_OVERLAP
```

The exact PGH incidence-profile construction remains project-specific in presentation, but its mathematical role—quotienting contextually indistinguishable presentations—is not an isolated foundational discovery.

### L2 — Rewriting and confluence

Church–Rosser, Newman, Knuth–Bendix, and modern term-rewriting theory establish that different derivational paths can be forced to converge, that canonical representatives can be produced under suitable conditions, and that equational reasoning can be converted into rewrite/coherence machinery.

PGH implication:

> “Different grammatical derivations should agree” is not by itself a new foundational principle. The substantive question is which rewrites/equivalences are justified and what their physical interpretation would be.

### L3 — Categorical coherence

Mac Lane's 1963 work is especially close to the project’s conditional parse-coherence result. It explicitly studies associativity given by natural isomorphism and conditions under which iterated products have a unique canonical comparison.

```text
PGH-DER-0003 = DIRECT_THEOREM_FAMILY_OVERLAP
```

This is the strongest prior-art correction produced by the landscape. The project must not present “parse coherence implies associativity/coherent association” as a uniquely PGH mathematical discovery.

### L4 — Operads and multicategories

May and Leinster supply mature mathematics for variable-arity operations, tree presentations, substitution, symmetric/nonsymmetric structure, and higher composition.

```text
PGH-Q-0014 = EXISTING_MATHEMATICAL_TOOLKIT_AVAILABLE
```

The open PGH question is no longer whether variable-arity compositional formalisms exist. They plainly do. The open question is whether any such formalism is physically privileged and whether equivalence between presentations can be justified without desired-result dependence.

### L5 — Algebraic theories, Lawvere theories, and monads

Lawvere, Eilenberg–Moore, and Manes show that syntax, operations, free constructions, algebraic semantics, and interpretation can be organized at a level abstracted from a single concrete term presentation.

```text
PGH-DER-0004 = SUBSTANTIAL_PRIOR_ART_NEIGHBORHOOD
PGH-Q-0015 = MATHEMATICALLY_REFRAMED_NOT_RESOLVED
```

The project’s projection/factorization language is therefore best treated as a local research scaffold inside a well-developed mathematical landscape.

### L6 — Graphical calculi

Joyal–Street and Selinger show that diagrammatic presentations can support rigorous invariant reasoning, with topological/combinatorial equivalences corresponding to algebraic equality or canonical morphisms under specified categorical structure.

This provides a powerful comparison class for PGH-Q-0008 and PGH-Q-0015.

### L7 — Theoretical equivalence and duality in physics

Weatherall, Barrett–Halvorson, and De Haro/Butterfield show that there is no single automatic criterion for “same physical theory.” Definitional, categorical, Morita, interpretational, and duality-based equivalences answer different questions and require attention to interpretation and excess structure.

This directly reinforces the current PGH failure:

```text
PGH-FAIL-0004 = CONSISTENT_WITH_EXISTING_EQUIVALENCE_LITERATURE
PGH-Q-0016 = LIVE_AND_NONTRIVIAL
```

Representation invariance alone does not uniquely specify what content should be retained.

### L8 — Structural realism

Worrall, Ladyman, and French establish a major philosophical tradition in which structural continuity, relations, laws, symmetry, and modal structure are treated as ontologically or epistemically central.

PGH is therefore **not** distinctive merely because it privileges relations/structure over object labels.

A key boundary remains:

```text
STRUCTURAL_REALISM != PHYSICAL_GRAMMAR_HYPOTHESIS
```

Structural realism does not by itself identify physical possibility with the well-formed expressions of a compact generative grammar.

### L9 — Categorical/process approaches to physics

Abramsky–Coecke, Coecke–Kissinger, and Chiribella–D’Ariano–Perinotti demonstrate that compositional/process structure and compact operational principles can carry serious physical content and derive nontrivial consequences.

This is strong novelty-risk evidence against any PGH claim of originality based only on “physics from composition” or “physics from compact structural principles.”

It does not establish strong PGH because these programs begin with specific categorical/operational/informational primitives and do not identify all physical law with linguistic/grammatical well-formedness.

### L10 — Formal language and generative grammar

Chomsky and standard formal-language theory establish the mathematical notion of grammars generating exactly a class of well-formed expressions and show that grammars have distinct generative powers.

This validates the project’s use of “grammar” as a legitimate formal analogy/tool, but it also imposes a terminology discipline:

```text
FORMAL_GENERATIVE_GRAMMAR = KNOWN_MATHEMATICAL_CONCEPT
PHYSICAL_IDENTITY_WITH_GRAMMAR = NOT_ESTABLISHED_BY_FORMAL_LANGUAGE_THEORY
```

### L11 — Structuralism, invariance, and identity

Shapiro, Awodey, and the Univalent Foundations Program provide strong prior art for identity by structural position, invariance, and the close treatment of equivalence/isomorphism and identity.

```text
PGH-DER-0001/0002/0004 = PHILOSOPHICAL_AND_FOUNDATIONAL_PRIOR_ART_NEIGHBORHOOD
```

Again, the physical interpretation remains unearned.

### L12 — Nearby foundational hypotheses

Five nearby families are especially important:

1. **Constructor theory** — possible/impossible transformations as fundamental explanatory objects.
2. **Operational/informational reconstructions** — quantum structure derived from compact principles.
3. **Laws as constraints** — lawhood represented as modal constraint, including non-dynamical/all-at-once forms.
4. **Ontic structuralism** — structure/laws/symmetries given fundamental ontological weight.
5. **Mathematical Universe Hypothesis** — the physical world identified with mathematical structure.

These create significant overlap pressure on broad versions of PGH.

The source search did not identify, within the frozen landscape, a source making the exact conjunction:

> there is a compact, representation-independent generative grammar whose well-formed structures, modulo physical equivalence, are exactly the physically possible structures, with fundamental laws being the constraints of that grammar rather than an additional law set.

This **must not** be converted into a novelty claim. Failure to find an exact formulation is only a landscape result.

## Scientific consequence of the landscape

The source intake does not change `PGH-GRAM-0002` and does not validate strong PGH.

It does change the **recommended scientific sequencing**.

The next step should not be another free-form axiom invention. It should be a bounded **prior-art overlap and residual-novelty adjudication** using this frozen corpus, with special focus on:

1. which PGH derivations are rediscoveries/local reformulations of standard results;
2. whether `PGH-Q-0014` can be narrowed using operad/multicategory machinery;
3. whether `PGH-Q-0015` can be taxonomized using known notions of theory/presentation equivalence;
4. whether `PGH-Q-0016` survives as a genuinely distinct research burden;
5. whether strong PGH remains meaningfully distinguishable from structural realism + laws-as-constraints + constructor/process/reconstruction programs + mathematical-universe theses.

No physical bridge should be attempted before that adjudication.
