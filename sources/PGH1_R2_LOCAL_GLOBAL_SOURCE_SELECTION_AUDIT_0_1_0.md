## Selection rules

A source was accepted only when it materially contributed to one of the preregistered SG1/SG2 roles: core extension/gluing machinery, a physical local-to-global obstruction case, formal global/boundary architecture, or a strong negative control on law-smuggling.

Keyword overlap alone was insufficient.

## Rejected/deferred candidates

### C16 — Abramsky, Barbosa, Mansfield, *Contextual Fraction as a Measure of Contextuality* (2017)

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REDUNDANT
```

Strong work, but primarily quantifies contextuality after the core local-to-global obstruction architecture is already represented by SRC-004/005 and the modern review SRC-009. It adds little to the present mechanism question.

### C17 — resource-theory review of contextuality (2019)

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REMOTE_RELEVANCE
```

Useful for contextuality as an operational resource, but the present intake concerns the origin of global nonextendability rather than downstream resource conversion.

### C18 — Price and Wharton, *Disentangling the Quantum World* (2015)

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REDUNDANT
```

Relevant retrocausal discussion, but Wharton–Argaman 2020 is a later, stronger authoritative review for the same SG2 mapping purpose.

### C19 — Wharton, *Lagrangian-Only Quantum Theory* (2013 preprint)

```text
DECISION = DEFER_WEAK_AUTHORITY_OR_MODEL_SPECIFIC
```

Directly relevant as a proof-of-principle model, but model-specific and weaker as a lane-defining authority than the accepted RMP review. It may be revisited if a future gate needs a concrete retrocausal toy model.

### C20 — Oeckl, *Probabilities in the General Boundary Formulation* (2006/2007)

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REDUNDANT
```

Useful exposition, but the 2008 foundational paper contains the broader axiomatic and probability structure needed here.

### C21 — Aharonov and Vaidman, *The Two-State Vector Formalism: An Updated Review* (2007)

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REDUNDANT
```

The ABL 1964 primary paper is sufficient for the two-boundary mechanism at this intake scope. The review can be revisited if detailed TSVF machinery becomes relevant.

### C22 — later structural/nonembeddability taxonomy of contextuality

```text
DECISION = REJECT_REMOTE_RELEVANCE
```

Interesting classification work, but the accepted modern RMP review plus the sheaf, graph, and hypergraph primary sources already supply a stronger representative map of the obstruction family.
