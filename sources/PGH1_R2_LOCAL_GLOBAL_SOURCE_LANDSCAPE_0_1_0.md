## SG1 — generic local-to-global extension machinery

Mac Lane and Moerdijk provide the standard mathematical background: sheaves organize local data and the conditions under which compatible local information glues to global information.

Vorob'ev is especially important for PGH because the extension problem predates the quantum contextuality application. His result studies consistent families of probability measures and gives structural conditions under which every such family extends to a global measure.

This establishes:

```text
LOCAL_COMPATIBILITY_DOES_NOT_GENERALLY_IMPLY_GLOBAL_EXTENDABILITY
GLOBAL_EXTENDABILITY_CAN_DEPEND_ON_COMBINATORIAL_COVER_STRUCTURE
LOCAL_TO_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION_IS_NOT_INTRINSICALLY_QUANTUM
```

That makes the mechanism more promising than simply importing a quantum law into a grammar.

But no physical credit follows yet. One must still explain why the relevant cover, local domains, and compatibility relations are the physically correct ones.

## SG1 — physical comparison through Bell/contextuality

Fine's theorem turns a Bell-type physical scenario into an extension problem: under the conditions treated there, existence of a single joint probability distribution is equivalent to Bell inequalities and corresponding hidden-variable conditions.

Abramsky and Brandenburger generalize this structure using measurement covers and sheaf language. Contextuality/nonlocality appear as obstructions to global sections over locally compatible empirical data.

This gives PGH a concrete model of **derived global impossibility**:

```text
LOCAL_EMPIRICAL_CONTEXTS = EACH_DEFINED
OVERLAP_COMPATIBILITY = SATISFIED
GLOBAL_NONCONTEXTUAL_ASSIGNMENT = MAY_NOT_EXIST
```

The physical no-go result is not obtained by listing the forbidden global assignments one by one. It arises from incompatibility of global extension with the pattern of local data.

The cohomology work then supplies computable obstruction witnesses. Its limitation is equally instructive: a particular cohomological obstruction is sufficient but not necessary, so the choice of obstruction detector must not be confused with the underlying contextuality condition.

## SG1 — R2 opportunity and firewall

## SG2 — global constraint form is scientifically legitimate

The source lane confirms that physical theories need not be represented only as initial-data-plus-forward-evolution systems.

Adlam provides the broad philosophical framework in which laws may be constraints on complete possibilities rather than solely evolution rules.

## SG2 — decisive negative control from action formulations

Feynman's path formulation is the essential firewall source.

A theory can be global over complete spacetime paths and still have the physical law inserted directly through the action-dependent phase assigned to each path.

Therefore:

```text
GLOBAL_FORMULATION != GRAMMAR_DERIVED_LAW
ALL_AT_ONCE != LAW_EXHAUSTION
VARIATIONAL_OR_PATH_STRUCTURE != NON_SMUGGLED_EXCLUSION
```

The same warning applies to any future PGH global constraint functional. If one is free to choose a functional `K[h]` so that exactly the desired histories satisfy `K[h]=0`, then the functional is simply another universal target encoder.

This is the SG2 analogue of `PGH-FAIL-0001`.
