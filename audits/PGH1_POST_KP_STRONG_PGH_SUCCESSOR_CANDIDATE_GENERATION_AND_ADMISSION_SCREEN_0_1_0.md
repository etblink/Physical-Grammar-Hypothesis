## Why the screen does not return zero

The post-Kp admission standard does not require a meta-derivation of every primitive grammar. It requires explicit target-free identity, selectivity, bridge, scope, instantiation, failure cost, quarantine and reproducibility.

A fixed sparse DAG is allowed to be a primitive grammar component when its selective wiring is explicit candidate content rather than hidden background.

The exhaustive two-edge connected three-role DAG lane therefore yields several admissible **hypotheses** once paired with:

- a full-model-class causal-ontology-neutral joint-distribution bridge;
- universal scope `S=TRUE`;
- the same outcome-neutral ordered three-role instantiation protocol for every member;
- explicit known-result quarantine;
- costly failure/revision rules.

Admission is not empirical support.
