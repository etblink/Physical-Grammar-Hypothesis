## Executive result

The SG3 corpus materially narrows the PGH research claim.

The following are established prior-art territories and receive no PGH novelty weight:

1. abstract frameworks capable of treating many logics without commitment to one concrete logic;
2. metalanguages capable of representing object-logical syntax, judgments, rules, and proofs;
3. abstract translations between distinct logics preserving suitable semantic/proof structure;
4. categorical/type-theoretic structures from which logical operations and inference principles can be generated or interpreted conditionally;
5. structural/substructural proof systems in which changing exchange, weakening, contraction, order, or related structural principles changes derivability and the resulting logic.

No exact-wording distinction preserves novelty for these ingredients.

The corpus does **not**, however, discharge the narrower anchored-PGH R2 burden:

\[
\boxed{\text{What non-result-directed principle generates the selective structural package itself?}}
\]

The mature meta-logical frameworks predominantly organize, encode, translate, or classify logics relative to supplied structural data. They do not provide a general physically privileged selector of that data.


Thus the PGH meta-language-origin problem cannot be solved merely by adopting an institution-like invariance criterion.

\[

\]

This is valuable machinery, not yet an independently selected successor grammar.

## F4 — structural / substructural proof theory

```text
STRUCTURAL_RULES_MATERIALLY_AFFECT_DERIVABILITY = ESTABLISHED_PRIOR_ART

```

Gentzen, Lambek, Girard, Belnap, Restall, Došen, and the survey literature make the role of structural context explicit.

Exchange, contraction, weakening, ordering, resource sensitivity, and related structural conditions are not merely typography. Altering them can change the logic.

This strongly supports the PGH bookkeeping conclusion that the structural package carries selective information.

But the plurality of coherent substructural systems also prevents the inference:

```text
STRUCTURAL_RULES_ARE_DEEP => ONE_STRUCTURAL_RULE_PACKAGE_IS_FORCED
```

## Residual statement

The surviving source-disciplined target is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Given only a law-free empirical reference boundary,}\
&\text{find a representation-robust generative principle that}\
&\text{produces the selective structural/signature/type/equation}\
&\text{package itself without target-result input, and show that}\
&\text{the resulting grammar performs substantive physical selection}\
&\text{without an independent law set.}
\end{aligned}}
\]

This has two separable requirements:

```text
R2A_STRUCTURAL_PACKAGE_GENERATION = UNSATISFIED
R2B_PHYSICAL_LAW_EXHAUSTION_FROM_GENERATED_PACKAGE = UNSATISFIED
```

The earlier R2 burden `PGH-Q-0017` remains open and contains both requirements at a higher level.

## Why outcome A does not pass

The SG3 corpus heavily overlaps PGH's meta-language discussion, but it does not make the anchored physical-law-exhaustion problem disappear.

A neutral logical framework can encode many logics without explaining which structural package, if any, is physically instantiated.

Therefore the entire PGH residue is not subsumed by meta-logic prior art at this source scope.

## Why outcome C does not pass

Categorical logic and type-theoretic frameworks are genuine structure-to-logic routes, but the base categorical/type structure remains supplied rather than independently selected by accepted PGH structure.

Promoting one now would violate the preregistered no-selection rule.

```text
CATEGORICAL_LOGIC_PRIORITY = HIGH_AS_FORMAL_RESOURCE
CATEGORICAL_LOGIC_AS_SUCCESSOR_GRAMMAR = NOT_QUALIFIED
```

The same applies to LF, institution theory, linear logic, display logic, and other sourced formalisms.

## Scientific consequence

The project should stop asking for a generic **meta-language**. Mature logic already has many.

The next frontier is the origin of the **structural package** that makes one metalanguage selective rather than neutral.

This is narrower than the original PGH and narrower than `PGH-FAIL-0017`:

\[
\text{neutral framework}
+\boxed{\Theta}
\to
\text{selective rule language},
\]

where `Theta` may encode structural rules, monoidal/cartesian properties, typing rules, equations, resource discipline, or related constraints.

PGH must either generate `Theta` from a still-smaller non-result-directed principle or acknowledge it as an independent substantive law input.
