## Executive result

The preregistered empty/full theorem is mathematically correct, but the gate's proposed interpretation of its premise as **outcome neutrality** does not survive internal PGH consistency review.

The project already canonically distinguishes:

```text
RELABELING_COVARIANCE = REPRESENTATION_NEUTRALITY
FULL_PERMUTATION_AUTOMORPHY = SUBSTANTIVE_HOMOGENEITY
```

in `PGH-FAIL-0002` and `PGH-DER-0002`.

The same distinction applies here.

A cross-type structure can be relabeled covariantly: when labels are renamed, the relation is transported with the presentation. Representation neutrality does not require the relation to remain the identical subset of one fixed carrier under every independent context and record permutation.

Therefore outcome A does not pass, even though its theorem component is valid.

## T1 — full independent automorphy theorem

Let nonempty sets `C` and `R` carry the full independent permutation action

\[
S_C\times S_R\curvearrowright C\times R.
\]

The action is transitive.

Hence a relation

\[
S\subseteq C\times R
\]

that is fixed by every independent permutation is necessarily

\[
S=\varnothing
\]

or

\[
S=C\times R.
\]

```text
T1_FORMAL_THEOREM = PASS
T1_AS_LABEL_OR_OUTCOME_NEUTRALITY = FAIL
```

The formal theorem is recorded as `PGH-DER-0022` with explicit conditional scope.

## T2 — deterministic control

For nonempty `C` and `|R|>1`, no function

\[
f:C\to R
\]

can satisfy

\[
f(\sigma c)=\tau f(c)
\]

for all independently chosen `sigma` and `tau`.

Setting `sigma=id` would require each `f(c)` to be fixed by every permutation of `R`, which is impossible for `|R|>1`.

```text
T2 = PASS_CONDITIONAL_ON_THE_SAME_STRONG_AUTOMORPHY_NOTION
```

The `|R|=1` degenerate exception is explicitly preserved.

## C1 — covariance versus automorphy

Suppose a presentation includes a nontrivial typed relation

\[
T\subseteq C\times R.
\]

Under bijective relabelings

\[
\sigma:C\to C',\qquad \tau:R\to R',
\]

representation covariance requires the renamed presentation to carry

\[
T'=(\sigma\times\tau)[T].
\]

It does not require `T'=T` as labeled subsets of one fixed carrier.

Thus nontrivial cross-type structure is compatible with representation neutrality.

This directly triggers the preregistered outcome-D condition: the chosen definition of outcome neutrality was too strong for the intended bridge problem.

## C2 — coupled-action escape

If an additional bijection

\[
\phi:C\to R
\]

is supplied, the graph of `phi` can be invariant under a coupled action.

This is a useful witness that the empty/full theorem is not a general theorem against nontrivial cross-type structure.

But `phi` is extra structure. If it is chosen because its graph matches a desired response pairing, the move fails the no-smuggling test.

## C3 — stabilizer universal-encoding control

For any target relation `S`, its setwise stabilizer

\[
H_S=\{g:gS=S\}
\]

preserves it by definition.

Therefore:

```text
INVARIANT_UNDER_SOME_CHOSEN_GROUP = NOT_EXPLANATORY_BY_ITSELF
```

A symmetry/orbit structure chosen after the target can encode the target just as an unrestricted predicate or formation table can.

This control is preserved in `PGH-FAIL-0024`.

## Why outcome A does not pass

The mathematical theorem assumed by outcome A is true.

But outcome A interprets full independent automorphy as the correct notion of outcome neutrality. That interpretation contradicts the canonical PGH covariance/automorphy distinction.

Promoting A would reintroduce a known failed premise.

## Why outcome B does not pass

No nontrivial proper relation survives the strong full-automorphy premise. The gate therefore does not exhibit the B witness.

## Why outcome C does not pass

No current accepted grammar or anchor artifact already supplies a qualified non-result-directed cross-type orbit/typing structure.

## Why outcome D passes

The locked theorem is coherent, but the proposed identification

```text
OUTCOME_NEUTRALITY = FULL_INDEPENDENT_AUTOMORPHY
```

is too strong and conceptually misaligned with the project's established representation standard.

The correct representation condition is covariance.

The correct semantic anti-smuggling condition should instead ask whether any supplied cross-type typing/interface structure remains compatible with multiple empirically incompatible response laws while held fixed.
