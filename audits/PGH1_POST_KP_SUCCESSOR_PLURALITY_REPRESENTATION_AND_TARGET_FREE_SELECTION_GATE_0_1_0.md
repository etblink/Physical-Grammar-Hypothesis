## Representation adjudication

At the full candidate level, they do not collapse. Candidate identity now includes `J` and `I`, and `I` gives the three empirical roles distinct metadata meanings. No such symmetry has been qualified.

## Why common-target testing is preferable

All five packages share:

- the same three ordered empirical roles;
- the same finite-record interface class;
- the same metadata-only target-discovery architecture;
- universal physical scope at the candidate scope layer;
- a single independence restriction per package.

One target can therefore confront the entire frozen family without candidate-specific target shopping.

A later protocol must account for the fact that five hypotheses are being tested and must state how outcomes update each candidate and the family.
