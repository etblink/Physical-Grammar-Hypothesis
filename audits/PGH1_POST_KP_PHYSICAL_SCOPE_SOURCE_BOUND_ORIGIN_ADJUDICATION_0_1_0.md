## Executive result

The SG5 corpus resolves the main ambiguity left by the post-Kp architecture gate.

Restricted domains of validity are not inherently post-hoc, arbitrary, or scientifically defective. Mature science often earns restricted applicability prospectively through explicit assumptions, scale hierarchies, effective degrees of freedom, validation conditions, causal/environmental structure, or model-to-target relations.

But that ordinary-science legitimacy does **not** by itself satisfy strong PGH.

For strong PGH, the physical information that decides where a grammar applies remains part of the law-exhaustion burden. If scale structure, symmetry, coupling regime, causal/environmental differences, or other substantive physical facts independently decide `S`, then the project has not explained those facts merely by keeping `G` compact inside the selected domain.

The source-bound result therefore strengthens `PGH-FAIL-0037` rather than removing it.

At the same time, the corpus does not prove that every restricted scope must depend on independent physical law. A grammar/bridge-internal scope mechanism (F1) and a narrow genuinely law-free semantic/reference condition (F2) remain logically open. Neither is currently physically qualified.

## Source-bound lane findings

### SG5-L1 — model/target applicability

The Morrison/Morgan, Giere, Maki, and Weisberg lane supports a consistent distinction:

```text
FORMAL_MODEL_EXISTENCE != WARRANTED_TARGET_RELATION
MODEL_TARGET_APPLICATION = ASSUMPTION_AND_CONTEXT_BEARING
TARGET_RELATION_CAN_BE_PROSPECTIVE
FORMAL_STRUCTURE_ALONE_DOES_NOT_SELECT_ALL_OR_ONE_PHYSICAL_TARGETS
```

This weighs against any claim that F1 earns physical applicability merely because a grammar has an elegant internal signature, typing structure, or model class.

It does **not** refute F1 in principle. A future PGH candidate could derive its own applicability relation. But the derivation must be exhibited; the source corpus provides no general theorem by which internal formal structure automatically acquires physical reference.

### SG5-L2 — effective theory and regime validity

Wilson/Kogut, Appelquist/Carazzone, Weinberg, Polchinski, Georgi, and Burgess establish strong ordinary-science examples of principled restricted scope.

The relevant conditions include:

```text
SCALE_HIERARCHY
MOMENTUM_OR_ENERGY_REGIME
HEAVY_LIGHT_DEGREES_OF_FREEDOM
DECOUPLING
SYMMETRIES
CUTOFF
RG_FLOW
POWER_COUNTING
EXPANSION_PARAMETER
```

These are prospectively specifiable and failure-exposing. They decisively show that restricted scope need not be result-selected.

They also decisively show why F3 does not automatically satisfy strong PGH: the scope conditions are substantive physical structure. When those facts are independently supplied to decide applicability, they instantiate the law-relocation concern unless the PGH candidate itself accounts for them.

### SG5-L3 — validation, discrepancy, predictive uncertainty

The Oberkampf/Trucano, Kennedy/O'Hagan, and Roy/Oberkampf lane establishes:

```text
FIT != ADEQUACY
CALIBRATION != VALIDATION
VALIDATION != UNRESTRICTED_EXTRAPOLATION
MODEL_DISCREPANCY_MAY_SURVIVE_BEST_FIT
PREDICTIVE_SCOPE_REQUIRES_UNCERTAINTY_AND_APPLICATION_CONTEXT
```

This supports the project's anti-rescue firewall. A failure may legitimately motivate model revision or scope revision in later science, but that revision cannot be credited retroactively to the failed candidate.

It also prevents F2 from defining scope as a disguised residual or fit criterion.

### SG5-L4 — transportability and invariance

Pearl/Bareinboim, Peters/Buhlmann/Meinshausen, and Rubenstein et al. establish that cross-domain validity can be formal, prospective, and failure-exposing.

But the formal transfer machinery is conditional on substantive structure:

```text
DOMAIN_DIFFERENCE_ASSUMPTIONS
CAUSAL_GRAPH_OR_SELECTION_STRUCTURE
ENVIRONMENT_SET
INVARIANCE_ASSUMPTIONS
INTERVENTION_SEMANTICS
CROSS_LEVEL_TRANSFORMATION
```

Thus a higher-order rule can control application without being arbitrary, but it does not select the relevant physical facts from nothing. This directly constrains both F3 and F4.

### SG5-L5 — semantic and partial-structural applicability

The da Costa/French and Bueno/French/Ladyman lane supports the possibility of partial, relational, structurally articulated application without literal identity or total isomorphism.

That keeps a narrow F2 open:

```text
SEMANTIC_OR_REFERENCE_CONTACT_CAN_BE_PARTIAL
SEMANTIC_MAPPING_NEED_NOT_ITSELF_BE_A_RESPONSE_LAW
```

But the same sources also preserve the key firewall:

```text
EMPIRICAL_STRUCTURE_IS_NOT_GENERATED_BY_FORMALISM_ALONE
APPLICATION_REMAINS_TARGET_DEPENDENT
SEMANTIC_RELATION_DOES_NOT_AUTOMATICALLY_SELECT_A_UNIQUE_PHYSICAL_DOMAIN
```

Therefore F2 receives no strong-PGH scope credit if its supposedly semantic conditions decide which substantive physical regularities are allowed to count as governed by the grammar.
