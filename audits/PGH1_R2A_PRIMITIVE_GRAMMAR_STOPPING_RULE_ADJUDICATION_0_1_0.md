## Executive result

The post-meta-language `R2A` requirement was useful as a discovery program but became too strong when interpreted as a mandatory truth-condition requiring every selective primitive to be generated by a deeper primitive.

Canonical anchored PGH is instead of the form

\[
A_{ref}+G\longrightarrow P,
\]

where `G` is the candidate generative grammar. The hypothesis requires substantive physical selection to be generated by `G` rather than by an external independent law set. It does not require `G` itself to be generated from a meta-grammar.

Therefore a declared primitive compositional doctrine may be admitted as a **candidate grammar**, but only under a strict anti-smuggling and nontriviality standard.

## T1 — canonical-text consistency

`PGH0_ANCHORED_STRONG_PGH_SPECIFICATION_0_1_0.md` defines `G` as the candidate generative grammar and forbids an independent substantive law set outside `G`.

It does not contain a requirement

```text
META_GRAMMAR -> G
```

or

```text
EVERY_PRIMITIVE_OF_G_MUST_BE_DERIVED.
```

`NONTRIVIALITY_TESTS.md` instead evaluates whether a candidate grammar is formally defined, non-universal, non-smuggled, exclusionary, compressive, representation-disciplined, consequence-bearing, and failure-exposing.

Thus mandatory meta-derivation is an added strengthening, not a canonical PGH truth-condition.

## T2 — primitive does not mean exempt

A declared primitive may still fail PGH badly.

### Reject control: extensional table

A primitive table `F=S` or support predicate `P_R` that simply lists or names the target admissibility set fails the universal-encoding/no-smuggling burden.

### Reject control: post-target doctrine choice

A structural doctrine chosen because its consequences reproduce a desired physical law fails pre-target fixation and no-smuggling.

### Candidate control: compact structural doctrine

A compact, precisely specified structural doctrine fixed before evaluating target consequences may enter candidacy if it generates broad consequences not definitionally identical to the postulates.

It still receives no physical privilege merely by being compact or canonical.

## T3 — mandatory-regress test

Requiring a derivation of every primitive changes the hypothesis into

\[
\cdots\to M_2\to M_1\to G\to P
\]

with no stopping criterion supplied by canonical PGH.

The current project has not proved such a hierarchy impossible, but neither may it impose that hierarchy as an unstated acceptance condition.

This overconstraint is preserved as `PGH-FAIL-0021`.

## T4 — candidacy is not confirmation

A primitive grammar that passes the admissibility standard earns at most a candidate-level status such as

```text
NONTRIVIAL_FORMAL_CANDIDATE
PHYSICAL_BRIDGE_UNESTABLISHED
```

It does not thereby earn:

```text
UNIQUE_FUNDAMENTAL_STATUS
PHYSICAL_PRIVILEGE
R2B_PASS
EMPIRICAL_CONFIRMATION
```

Multiple competing primitive grammars may be preregistered and compared.

## T5 — primitive grammar admissibility standard

The adjudication creates `PGH-OBJ-0020`, a candidate standard requiring all of the following at minimum:

```text
P0_FORMAL_DEFINABILITY = PASS
P1_DECLARED_PRIMITIVES_AND_ASSUMPTION_ANCESTRY = PASS
P2_PRE_TARGET_FIXATION = PASS
P3_RESTRICTED_HYPOTHESIS_CLASS / UNIVERSAL_ENCODING_CONTROL = PASS

P5_GENERATIVE_COMPRESSION = PASS_OR_EXPLICITLY_TESTABLE
P6_NONTRIVIAL_EXCLUSION = PASS
P7_REPRESENTATION_DISCIPLINE = PASS_OR_PREREGISTERED_TEST
P8_LAW_FREE_SEMANTIC_BOUNDARY = PASS
P9_INDEPENDENT_CONSEQUENCE = PASS
P10_COUNTEREXAMPLE_EXPOSURE = PASS
P11_PHYSICAL_BRIDGE = SEPARATE_AND_NOT_YET_ASSUMED
```

No scalar score is permitted. A hard failure on universal encoding, target-result smuggling, or inability to expose counterexamples blocks candidacy.

## T6 — plurality control

Scientific candidacy does not require a unique derivation of one grammar.

A family of candidate grammars may be fixed independently and subjected to the same controls. Underdetermination at the formal stage is a reason for comparative testing, not a reason to manufacture a deeper selector after the fact.

## T7 — law exhaustion preserved

Outcome A changes only the stopping rule for `G`.

The central anchored claim remains:

\[
P=\operatorname{Select}_L(W(G),A_{ref})
\]

is the null whenever an independent law-like selector `L` still performs substantive physical selection.

Therefore the surviving burden remains:

```text
R2B = CAN_A_CANDIDATE_GRAMMAR_G_EXHAUST_SUBSTANTIVE_PHYSICAL_LAW_AFTER_A_REF?
STATUS = UNSATISFIED
```

## Supersession decision

`PGH-OBJ-0017` remains an important prior-art-subtracted historical target, but its statement

```text
R2A_IS_LOGICALLY_PRIOR_AND_MANDATORY
```

is superseded as a project-wide acceptance condition.

A new successor object is created:

```text
PGH-OBJ-0021 = POST_STOPPING_RULE_ANCHORED_R2_RESIDUAL
```

The successor preserves all no-smuggling and law-exhaustion requirements while allowing a declared primitive `G` to enter candidacy under `PGH-OBJ-0020`.

## Scientific consequence

The project is now allowed to do actual **candidate grammar science** without pretending a candidate has been uniquely derived.

The next operation should preregister a small family of structurally motivated candidate grammars **before physical-fit testing**, screen them against `PGH-OBJ-0020`, and reject any that remain universal encoders or lack independent formal exclusion.

Recommended:

```text
PGH1_R2_PRIMITIVE_GRAMMAR_CANDIDATE_FAMILY_SCREEN
```

No physical bridge should begin in that screen.
