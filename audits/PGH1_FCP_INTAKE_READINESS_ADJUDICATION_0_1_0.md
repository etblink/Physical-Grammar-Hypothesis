### RG2 — non-generic primitive/object class

**Verdict: PASS_FOR_INTAKE_WITH_CRITICAL_QUALIFICATION.**

The mathematical ingredients of `PGH-GRAM-0010` are not themselves novel physical primitives. A triangle latent-variable/source network with independent latent sources and local kernels is generic mathematical/statistical structure. PGH receives **zero physical credit** merely for using it.

The intake-relevant object is narrower and stronger: PGH-OBJ-0052 asserts that the grammar-generated proper observable class `T_ind` is the physical possibility constraint for every interface admitted by its frozen target-neutral protocol. The candidate therefore makes a physical law claim *using* generic mathematics rather than claiming the mathematics is physically privileged by existence alone.

```text
GENERIC_TRIANGLE_MODEL_EXISTS = NOT_PHYSICAL_EVIDENCE
T_IND_PROPER_OBSERVABLE_CLASS = FORMAL_RESULT
UNIVERSAL_PHYSICAL_CONSTRAINT_CLAIM = CANDIDATE_PHYSICAL_CONTENT
```

FCP may later decide that this physical claim is too thin, derivative, or better classified as a model-level postulate. That is exactly why intake/taxonomy is warranted rather than why it should be skipped.

### RG3 — coherent model/configuration domain

**Verdict: PASS.**

`T_ind` is one whole observable model class, not a target-fitted point model. The package forbids proper-subset selection, local-kernel selection, latent-cardinality tuning for fit, thresholding, binning, and state merging during instantiation.

An explicit observed distribution—the perfect common bit—is outside the class, so the domain is proper rather than universal.

### RG4 — Method-0.2.1 criterion-C plausibility

**Verdict: PASS_FOR_INTAKE_NOT_FINAL_ADMISSION.**

FCP Method 0.2.1 asks whether the candidate contains a physically interpreted law/constraint architecture that says what physical possibilities are, rather than merely representing possibilities supplied by another theory.

PGH-OBJ-0052 supplies a serious candidate for this burden:

```text
FOR EVERY I-ELIGIBLE PHYSICAL RECORD INTERFACE:
    p_emp(A,B,C) in T_ind
```

The semantic bridge does not choose one favorable member of `T_ind`; it transfers the whole class. The scope is universal over the prospectively frozen interface rule. Rejection of class membership is costly candidate failure.

Thus a physical constraint is actually asserted. Whether that constraint is sufficiently source-bound, physically meaningful, and framework-constitutive for final `C=PASS` remains FCP's independent job.

### RG5 — physical scope and interpretation burden

**Verdict: PASS.**

The package has both:

1. a source-origin-adjudicated `F0_UNIVERSAL` scope; and
2. a law-free whole-class semantic bridge `PGH-OBJ-0051`.

Universal scope is not empirical evidence. It is scientifically useful because it avoids a favorable target-specific scope selector and maximizes exposure to refutation.

The bridge is also deliberately ontology-light: latent sources need not be identified with real physical causes. That avoids one smuggling route, while leaving FCP free to ask whether the resulting physical interpretation is too weak for frameworkhood.

## 7. Why R2 and R3 are not controlling

### R2 — near ready

A named pre-FCP gap would be controlling if FCP could not even ask one of A–H without PGH first inventing new ontology, physical law content, scope, bridge, or falsification conditions.

That is no longer the state after `PGH-OBJ-0052`. The remaining gaps—target discovery, empirical validation, independent source audit, and taxonomy distinctness—are exactly the kinds of burdens an FCP intake/taxonomy operation is designed to assess rather than prerequisites that must be solved beforehand.

### R3 — not ready

R3 would require no stable FCP candidate object. The exact `(G,J,S,I)` package defeats that description.
