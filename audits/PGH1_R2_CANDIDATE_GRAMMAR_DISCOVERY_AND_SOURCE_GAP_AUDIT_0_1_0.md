The frozen corpus contains several mature mechanisms for formal exclusion and several mature physical-principle programs, but it does not yet contain a family that responsibly bridges those two roles for anchored R2.

The main positive result of this operation is therefore not a grammar selection. It is a sharper map of **where the gap lies**.

---

## 1. Discovery standard

Anchored R2 requires more than a formal rule that excludes something. A useful candidate principle family must offer a plausible route to:

```text
LAW_FREE_REFERENCE
+
INDEPENDENTLY_MOTIVATED_GRAMMATICAL_PRINCIPLE
->
SUBSTANTIVE_PHYSICAL_EXCLUSION
```

without either:

```text
A. encoding the exclusion extensionally in a table or freely chosen rule set;
or
B. importing an already substantive physical law and merely renaming it grammar.
```

This audit therefore distinguishes **formal exclusion power** from **R2 candidacy**.


Confluence, termination, and completion genuinely rule out classes of rewrite systems or derivational behavior without enumerating every forbidden path. This is mathematically nontrivial.

However, the rewrite **rules themselves** remain free input. For any target relation one may simply choose a rewrite presentation designed to encode it. Confluence then constrains consistency of the chosen rules rather than explaining why those rules have physical authority.

This family therefore reproduces the PGH-0 pattern at a more sophisticated level:

```text
FORMAL_PATH_CONSISTENCY = REAL
PHYSICAL_RESPONSE_OR_POSSIBILITY_SELECTION = NOT_DERIVED
```

No new source gap blocks that conclusion.


This is the strongest frozen evidence that compact principles can generate substantial physical structure.

But the principles used in reconstructions—purification, distinguishability, compression, local discriminability, continuity, simplicity, and related operational axioms—are already substantive claims about physical operations or information-processing possibilities.

They may be excellent physical principles. The PGH problem is different: if such a principle is simply inserted into `G` and renamed a grammatical production rule, law exhaustion becomes terminological rather than explanatory.

Therefore this family is an essential **positive control** for what successful derivation from compact principles looks like, but not yet a direct PGH grammar candidate.

## 10. Cross-lane result — the missing middle mechanism

The frozen families divide into two broad groups.

### Formal-side families

```text
F1_REWRITE_CONFLUENCE
F2_CATEGORICAL_COHERENCE
F3_UNIVERSAL_PROPERTIES
F4_OPERADIC_COMPOSITION
```

have strong non-table-driven formal exclusion machinery, but no current reason that their excluded formal structures coincide with physically impossible structures.

### Physical-side families

```text
F5_OPERATIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION
F6_CONSTRUCTOR_TASK_POSSIBILITY
```

have clear physical content, but their physically substantive principles or possibility assignments enter as premises.

F7 and F8 sit between these groups but are not sufficiently developed in the frozen corpus to supply the missing mechanism.

The discovery target is therefore not "more formalism" or "more physical axioms." It is a principle by which a compact, independently motivated consistency structure can turn locally admissible ingredients into **globally impossible combinations** without an extensional impossibility table.

## 11. Concrete source gap SG1 — local-to-global compatibility obstruction

Repository-wide search of the frozen project state found no dedicated source lane for:

```text
SHEAF_OR_PRESHEAF_GLUING
DESCENT_OR_LOCAL_TO_GLOBAL_CONSISTENCY
GLOBAL_SECTION_OBSTRUCTIONS
CONTEXTUALITY_AS_LOCAL_COMPATIBILITY_WITH_GLOBAL_NONREALIZABILITY
CONSTRAINT_SYSTEMS_WITH_LOCALLY_CONSISTENT_BUT_GLOBALLY_INCONSISTENT_ASSIGNMENTS
```

This mechanism is scientifically relevant because it has the exact abstract shape R2 needs to investigate:

\[
\text{all local pieces admissible}
\centernot\Rightarrow
\text{one globally admissible structure}.
\]

A failure of global extendability can be a genuine derived exclusion rather than an entry in a forbidden-case table.

This audit does **not** assert that sheaves, contextuality, descent, or any particular formalism is the PGH grammar. It establishes only that the current corpus lacks the sources needed to evaluate this mechanism responsibly.

```text
SG1_STATUS = CONCRETE_SOURCE_GAP
SG1_PRIORITY = HIGH
```

## 12. Concrete source gap SG2 — formal global-constraint mechanisms

The frozen corpus contains a strong conceptual laws-as-constraints source but insufficient primary/technical coverage of formal global or all-at-once constraint mechanisms suitable for the R2 firewall.

The missing audit question is:

> Under what general formal conditions does a compact global consistency requirement exclude whole classes of histories/configurations without the specific target physical law being encoded extensionally in the constraint definition?

The future lane must explicitly distinguish:

```text
GENERIC_GLOBAL_CONSISTENCY_MECHANISM
from
A_SPECIFIC_ACTION_OR_CONSTRAINT_FUNCTIONAL_THAT_ALREADY_IS_THE_PHYSICAL_LAW
```
